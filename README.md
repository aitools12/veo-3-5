## What is Veo 3.5

Veo 3.5 is a browser-based AI video creation platform. It accepts text prompts and image inputs and generates short-form video clips optimized for social media, product demos, creative projects, and rapid prototyping. The tool runs entirely in the browser, requiring no local GPU or software installation.

## Why use Veo 3.5

Traditional video production requires editing skills, rendering time, and expensive hardware. Veo 3.5 removes those barriers. Users can generate usable video clips in seconds from a simple text description, iterate on prompts, and download results directly. It is designed for marketers, content creators, product teams, and anyone who needs quick video assets without a production pipeline.

## How Veo 3.5 works

1. **Enter a prompt**: Describe the scene you want to generate in natural language.
2. **Optional image input**: Upload a reference image for image-to-video generation.
3. **Generate and download**: The model processes the input and returns a video clip ready for download.
